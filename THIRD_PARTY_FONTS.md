## Surah name calligraphy (`drawable/surah_name_*.png`)

Rendered from the SVG glyphs at [gyenabubakar/surah-name-glyphs](https://github.com/gyenabubakar/surah-name-glyphs)
(a public collection of Qur'an chapter-name calligraphy released for use in Muslim apps),
re-colored to Noor's palette. Credit to the original glyph/font authors.
